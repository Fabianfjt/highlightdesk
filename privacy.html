Privacy Policy

Highlight Desk Studio · last updated 17 November 2026

Home · Terms of Service · Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

Highlight Desk Studio is a desktop tool operated by an individual creator for use with their own TikTok account. Contact: fabianfjt@gmail.com.

2. What data we use

  • Basic TikTok profile information (open ID, display name, avatar), to show which account is connected.
  • An access token from TikTok, used only to upload videos as drafts to that account.
  • The video files and texts that the user chooses to upload.

3. Where data is stored

All data stays on the operator's own computer. There is no server, no database and no analytics. The access token is stored in a local file that is not shared.

4. Sharing

Data is only sent to TikTok, through TikTok's official API, to upload drafts. We do not sell or share data with anyone else.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens and files can be deleted at any time by the operator. You can also revoke the tool's access in your TikTok settings, after which the token no longer works.

6. Your rights

You may ask for access to or deletion of your data by emailing fabianfjt@gmail.com. If you are in the EU, you also have the rights given by the GDPR, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

7. Children

The tool is not intended for anyone under 18.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest version.
